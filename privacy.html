Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026

This policy explains what information Rimu Coachworks collects when you visit www.rimucoachworks.com, book a consultation or write to us, and what we do with it. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Rimu Coachworks, Bridgeport, Connecticut.

What we collect

Information you give us. When you book a consultation or email the workshop, we receive what you choose to send: usually your name, email address, phone number if you include it, and details about the car or commission you have in mind.

Information collected automatically. When you visit the site, our advertising and analytics tools record information about your visit, such as the pages you view, the buttons you tap, the website or ad that brought you here, your general location (to the city level), and your browser and device type. This is collected through cookies and similar technologies, described below.

We do not ask for, and do not want, payment card details, government ID numbers or other sensitive information through this website.

How we use it

  • To arrange and hold your consultation, and to reply to your enquiries.
  • To prepare proposals and carry out any commission you go ahead with.
  • To understand how visitors find and use the site, and to measure whether our advertising works.
  • To show our advertising to people who have visited the site, on Google and its partner sites.

Cookies, Google Ads and Google Analytics

We use Google Ads and may use Google Analytics. These services place cookies in your browser and collect information about your visit and the actions you take, such as booking a consultation or tapping our email link. We use this to measure the results of our advertising, and Google may use it to show you our ads on other websites (remarketing). Google processes this information under its own policies: see how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

You can limit this tracking at any time:

  • Turn off personalized ads in Google's ad settings.
  • Install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
  • Opt out of interest-based ads from many companies at optout.aboutads.info.

Your browser may send a "Do Not Track" signal. Because there is no common standard for responding to it, the site does not change its behavior when it receives one. Where the law treats a Global Privacy Control signal as an opt-out request, we honor it.

Bookings through Calendly

Consultations are booked through Calendly, which collects the details you enter in the booking form on our behalf and sends us your booking. Calendly handles that information under its own privacy policy.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers who help us run the business, such as Google, Calendly, our website host and our email provider, and only as needed for them to do that work. We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect our rights, or as part of a sale or reorganization of the business.

We do not sell your personal information. Some privacy laws treat the use of advertising cookies for targeted advertising as a "sale" or "sharing" of information. You can opt out of that use with the tools above, or by emailing us.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and booking details for as long as we need them to deal with you, and for longer if you commission a car, for as long as we need records of that work and for legal, tax and accounting purposes. Advertising and analytics data is kept according to Google's retention settings.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it or delete it, or ask us to stop using it for advertising. Depending on where you live, including Connecticut, you may have legal rights to make these requests. Email us at the address below and we will respond within the time the law requires. If we turn down a request, you can ask us to reconsider by replying to our answer.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the information we hold. No website or email system is completely secure, so please do not send us anything sensitive by email.

Children

This website is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the effective date at the top.

Contact

For any question about this policy or your information, email commissions@rimucoachworks.com.
