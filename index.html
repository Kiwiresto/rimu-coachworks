The name
Rimu, New Zealand's native red timber

Most shops start with what you want to spend. We start with what the car should be, and then we build exactly that.

Rimu Coachworks takes on a small number of cars and takes each one as far as it can honestly go. Bare metal. Every system rebuilt or replaced. Paint, hide and brightwork finished by people who do nothing else.

Some of our cars are restomods: the original shape left alone, with modern power, brakes, steering and comfort underneath. Some are restorations taken past factory standard. Either way the brief is the same. Nothing is value-engineered, nothing is rushed, and nothing leaves until it has been driven hard enough to find what's wrong with it.

They are not inexpensive, and they are not quick. They are meant to be the best example of the car that exists.

The Name
RimuPronounced REE-moo

Rooted in New Zealand’s rich Māori tradition, the majestic Rimu tree is the offspring of Tāne-mahuta, god of the forests, whose separation of Earth and Sky brought light into the world. As living pillars holding up the heavens, these ancient forest giants inspire our company’s dedication to strength, heritage, and timeless craftsmanship in every classic restoration.

The Craft

Built by the people whose names go in the book.

No holds barred.

A car like this is not built by a production line. It is built by a handful of specialists, each the best we can find at one thing, working on one car at a time.

Every Rimu car leaves with a build book: the specification, the photographs from every stage, and the names of the people who did the work. The parts that came off it come with it too.

  • MetalBare-shell repair and fabrication, hand-formed where the factory stamped
  • PaintBlocked through repeated primer rounds before a drop of colour
  • HidePatterned, cut and sewn to the finished car, not to a template
  • SystemsNew wiring, cooling, fuel and climate, built to the car and hidden
  • SortingDyno tuning, alignment and 500 road miles before handover
How a commission works

From first meeting to handover

Five steps, in this order. Nothing is agreed on the phone and nothing is started without a signed, fixed-price proposal.

01

Consultation

A private appointment at the workshop or by video. We talk about the car, how you'll use it, and what it should feel like to drive.

By appointment
02

Proposal

A written proposal with the full specification, the programme and one fixed price. It covers the car, the parts and the labour, with no hourly overruns.

Fixed price
03

Sourcing

We find the best honest example of the car and tell you exactly what we found. The build clock starts when it arrives in the shop.

Donor included
04

The build

Teardown, metal, chassis, paint, systems and trim, photographed at every stage. Colour and hide samples come to you before anything is sprayed or cut.

Reported throughout
05

Shakedown & handover

Dyno tuning, alignment and 500 miles on the road to find and fix everything a new car does. You take delivery of a sorted car, with its build book.

500 miles
